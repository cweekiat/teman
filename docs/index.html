Five tabs

Simple enough for tired days.

Large text, big buttons, few words and soft colours. Built with Apple's design guidelines and Liquid Glass, and works at the largest text sizes.

1Today

Everything for today, in one place.

Open Teman and see what matters now.

  • Which cycle and day you're on, and when blood counts are usually lowest
  • Your next visit, with a countdown to leave and directions in Maps
  • Today's medicines, ticked off with one tap
  • A short, kind thought for the day
2My Care

Your plan, counted and explained.

Add your diagnosis and treatment by speaking, typing or taking a photo of your plan.

  • 27 cancer types and 30 common treatment plans explained simply, each with its source
  • The medicines, a usual cycle, and what to watch for
  • Questions for your doctor, a How I Feel diary and visit notes
  • Words: tap a term you heard and hear it explained
3Talk

Ask anything, by voice or text.

Teman listens, answers and reads replies aloud in a natural male or female voice.

  • Answers come from what's in the app: your plan, drug coverage, contacts and support
  • Add a photo of a label, bill or letter and Teman reads it
  • Runs with Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to a server
  • Gentle haptics as it listens and replies
4Binder

Medicines and papers, kept tidy.

Scan a medicine label or a letter. Teman reads it on the iPhone.

  • Medicine reminders with a Taken button on the notification
  • A second reminder 30 minutes later if you haven't tapped it
  • Scanned letters, reports, PDFs and bills in one place
  • Edit or delete anything, any time
5Help

The right help, a tap away.

995 and your care team at the top. Then everything else.

  • "I need to…": the right NCCS contact, with the words ready to say
  • Money and bills: Cancer Drug List, CPF, insurance, financial help
  • Meet others: support groups, talks, art and music
  • Breathe with Me, with voice, visuals and gentle vibration

Features

Small helps that add up.

Built from what patients and caregivers in Singapore say they need: understanding, keeping track, and speaking up.

Is my drug covered?

A yes, no or "check" answer for each drug in your plan, matched to your cancer, from MOH's Cancer Drug List.

Understand my bill

Scan a bill or open the HealthHub PDF. See subsidy, MediShield Life, insurance, MediSave and what's left for you.

Explained, with sources

27 cancer types and 30 treatment plans in plain words. Every medical sentence names where it came from.

Reminders that wait for you

Medicines, when to leave for a visit, and the day before each cycle. Tap Taken on the notification itself.

Ask Doctor

Questions ready for each visit, suggested for your treatment. Note the answers and follow-ups after.

How I Feel

Log symptoms and temperature in seconds. Charts and a summary to share with your doctor.

Calendar, your way

Bring visits in from your Calendar, or add them yourself. Leave Now opens directions in Maps.

Meet others, give back

NCCS and community support groups, talks and activities, plus ways to volunteer.

Breathe with Me

A calm, guided breath with a growing circle, a soft voice and gentle vibration.

Private by design

Your story stays on your iPhone.

No account. No server. No ads. No tracking. The developer never sees your information, because it never leaves your phone unless you share it.

What Teman does

  • Keeps everything on the iPhone, encrypted when it's locked
  • Locks with Face ID if you want, and hides medicine names on the Lock Screen
  • Reads text, speech and photos on the device
  • Lets you copy or delete all your data any time
  • Shows a source for every medical explanation

What Teman never does

  • Diagnose cancer or read results as good or bad
  • Choose, compare or rank treatments
  • Decide if you can join a clinical trial
  • Replace your doctor, nurse or pharmacist
  • Share or sell your information
