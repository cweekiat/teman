Teman Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Privacy Policy

In short

Teman keeps your information on your iPhone. The developer does not collect, receive, see, sell or share any of it. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your treatment plan, medicines, appointments, questions and answers, symptom log, documents and photos you scan, and your settings. They are protected by your iPhone's encryption, and by Face ID or your passcode if you turn on Teman's lock.

Permissions and why

Camera: to scan letters, bills and labels. Photos and Files: only the items you pick. Microphone and speech recognition: only while you are speaking to Teman. Calendar: only when you choose to import appointments, and only the events you tick are copied. Notifications: for reminders. Face ID: to lock Teman. You can turn each off in iPhone Settings.

Processing on your iPhone

Text recognition, speech recognition and AI answers run on your iPhone using Apple's frameworks. If you turn on “Apple voice service” for a language your iPhone can't recognise offline, your voice is sent to Apple for recognition under Apple's privacy policy. It is off unless you turn it on.

Sharing is your choice

Information leaves your iPhone only when you share it yourself, for example exporting a copy, sharing a summary with your doctor, calling a number or opening a link. iCloud backups of your iPhone, if you use them, are handled by Apple.

Your rights

You can see and edit everything in the app. Help › Privacy lets you make a full copy of your data and delete everything. Because the developer holds none of your data, there is nothing for the developer to access, correct or delete on your behalf.

Children

Teman is meant for adults and caregivers. A parent or guardian should set it up for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, Teman will show the new version and ask you to accept it. Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub page, without including any health information.
