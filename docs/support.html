Teman Support

In an emergency, call 995. If you are on cancer treatment and feel unwell, call the number your care team gave you. Teman is not a medical service.

Questions about the app

Open an issue at github.com/cweekiat/teman/issues. Please never post your health information or NRIC.

Common answers

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. Teman has no server. In the app, Help › Privacy lets you export or delete everything.

Reminders don't appear. Check Settings › Notifications › Teman, and any Focus mode. Don't rely on Teman as your only reminder.

Is a drug covered? Teman checks MOH's Cancer Drug List, but your hospital decides. Ask your Medical Social Worker.
