No medical advice

Teman does not give medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, recommend or compare treatments or doses, judge whether you can join a clinical trial, or tell you how serious a symptom is. Always follow your own doctor, nurse and pharmacist. Never delay or ignore their advice because of something in Teman.

No financial or insurance advice

Coverage answers (for example the Cancer Drug List check) are general information based on MOH's published list on the date shown. They are not a decision about your coverage. Your hospital, MOH, the CPF Board and your insurer decide what is covered and what you pay. Teman does not decide eligibility for any scheme.

Information can be wrong or out of date

Teman summarises public sources and shows them with links. Sources change, and summaries can be incomplete or wrong. Text written by the on-device AI can contain mistakes. Check anything important with your care team or the original source.

Not a medical device

Teman is a personal organiser and information tool. It has not been assessed or registered by HSA as a medical device.

Reminders may not arrive

Reminders depend on your iPhone's settings, battery and Focus modes, and may be late or not appear. Don't rely on Teman as your only reminder for medicines or appointments.

In an emergency

Teman is not an emergency service. Call 995 for an emergency ambulance. If you are on cancer treatment and feel unwell, call the number your care team gave you.
